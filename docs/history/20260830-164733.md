## Summary
`LoadedModel` now follows the required CRUD convention. `ModelRuntime` creates a pending collection item before loading, publishes updates when the status snapshot changes, publishes update on success, and publishes delete on failed/cancelled loading. `LoadedModel_Read` is the server-side source-of-truth read used during full page initialization. SignalR carries `LoadedModel_Create`, `LoadedModel_Update`, and `LoadedModel_Delete` snapshots; the Backends page subscribes to them and no longer polls during loading. The explicit naming and responsibility rules are documented in `.github/copilot-instructions.md`. Studio build, 37 Core tests, 26 Studio tests, and editor diagnostics passed.
